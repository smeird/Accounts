What these figures mean

This is an illustrative drawdown planner, not a guaranteed income or an annuity quote. The pot is intended to last to your chosen end age, with no inheritance reserve. Real returns vary, and losses early in retirement can materially change the outcome.

Daily income means annual income divided by 365.2425. Monthly contributions stay fixed in cash terms until retirement, arrive at month end, and stop at retirement. Annual fees apply to each pot; retirement assumes a rebalanced portfolio using its value-weighted net return. Withdrawals occur at month start. State Pension retains its purchasing power in this model; future policy is not predicted.

Valuations are treated as today’s starting balances, even when dated earlier. Refresh old values for a useful forecast. Income is before tax: income tax, tax-free lump sums, allowances, other assets and pension guarantees are not modelled.

Retirement age is distinct from pension access and State Pension ages. The default private access age is 57; change it only to match your scheme’s confirmed rules. Pension access rules.

Your pots stay separate from bank balances and transaction totals. Include Pensions in Backup & Restore to protect this workspace.

Sources and assumptions

    Update pensions → · Update mortgage → · Review account balances →

    Refresh old valuations in the source planners. Their recorded values are treated as today’s starting balances; this page does not roll old valuations forward. Closed accounts are excluded. Other assets and debts must exclude anything already in bank balances, pension pots or the mortgage. All other debts are deducted from liquid assets immediately; this model does not forecast separate debt repayments.

    Monthly modelling uses saved pension growth, fees, inflation, access age, State Pension and spending phases. Contributions and extra saving stay fixed in nominal pounds until retirement. Other income starts at your selected age, only during retirement, and maintains its purchasing power to the end age. It is not an asset. Pension access is enforced; early retirement needs liquid savings to bridge the gap. No State Pension entitlement is assumed.

    The saved regular mortgage payment and optional rate reset are used; saved lump sums and extra repayments are excluded so cash is not counted twice. Repayments before retirement are assumed funded from earnings, separately from extra saving. After retirement, mortgage payments are added to your living-cost target. A debt remaining at contractual maturity must be repaid in full; the model does not assume refinancing. Downsizing at retirement repays the mortgage first, then buys the replacement home and pays costs. A negative release needs funding. Replacement price follows the same growth as your existing home; moving costs rise with inflation.

    Income is before tax and does not model tax-free lump sums, contribution limits, benefits, investment volatility or care costs. Enter selling, buying, legal, moving, tax and early repayment charges in costs. No inheritance reserve is retained in spendable assets. The sustainable income is level or follows your saved Go-Go/Slow-Go/No-Go profile. An unmet target can exhaust assets before your selected end age.
